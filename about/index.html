About

A path through computation and mathematics

I grew up in India and moved across many cities, spending most of my pre-adult life in Punjab. My academic path began in Computer Science at IIIT Hyderabad before shifting toward the questions in pure mathematics that kept pulling at my attention.

Background

From computer science to arithmetic geometry

After undergraduate study at the International Institute of Information Technology, Hyderabad, I moved into pure mathematics through a master's program at the University of Warwick, where I wrote my thesis under Prof. John Cremona.

I then continued with master's study at IIT Kanpur. That route led me to the PhD program in Mathematics at the University of Illinois Chicago, where I work under Prof. Alina Carmen Cojocaru.
