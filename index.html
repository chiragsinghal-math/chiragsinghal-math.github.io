Chirag Singhal

I study number theory and arithmetic geometry at the University of Illinois Chicago, advised by Prof. Alina Carmen Cojocaru.

My current research focuses on lifting of elliptic curves and the distribution of Frobenius traces over primes of good reduction.

I am interested in how geometric objects encode arithmetic information and how those patterns vary across primes, fields, and families.
