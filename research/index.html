Research

Number theory with a geometric lens

My research interests are in number theory, especially arithmetic geometry. I am currently thinking about lifting of elliptic curves and the distribution of Frobenius traces over primes of good reduction.

Current Project

Lifting elliptic curves and tracking Frobenius traces

The central theme is how geometric objects carry arithmetic information, and how that information changes across primes, fields, and related families.
